How to research Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads

A sound review of Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads starts with identity, timing and later changes. A listing that leaves the active feed is not automatically a sale. Compare the former asking price, listing dates and time on market with any relist, pending status or later recorded deed before calling it expired or withdrawn.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Look for subsequent filings or listing changes before treating the first notice as current.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price. For additional context, consult the LienScraper property research guide.
