How to research Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads

Before outreach about Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads, confirm the record and the property's current status. A listing that leaves the active feed is not automatically a sale. Compare the former asking price, listing dates and time on market with any relist, pending status or later recorded deed before calling it expired or withdrawn.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Compare the named party with current ownership before planning outreach. For broader context, see the LienScraper property research guide.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price.
