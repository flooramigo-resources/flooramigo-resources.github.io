How to research Houston property lien leads

A sound review of Houston property lien leads starts with identity, timing and later changes. Identify the lien type, claimant, recording date and property description. Check for a later release or satisfaction and for other claims against the same parcel. A recorded amount is not necessarily the current payoff.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Look for subsequent filings or listing changes before treating the first notice as current.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price.
