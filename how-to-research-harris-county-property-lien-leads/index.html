How to research Harris County property lien leads

Before outreach about Harris County property lien leads, confirm the record and the property's current status. Identify the lien type, claimant, recording date and property description. Check for a later release or satisfaction and for other claims against the same parcel. A recorded amount is not necessarily the current payoff.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Compare the named party with current ownership before planning outreach.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price.
